## Anytime-valid / sequential statistics (e-values, e-processes, testing by betting, conformal martingales)

Target: PLAN §7 calibration layer — the strong-law-of-small-numbers gate, the STRONG-EMPIRICAL/CONJECTURE scoring of accumulating cases, the selector audit (§10), and cost-profile drift (§5). Verdict up front: the mechanism fits ONE place cleanly (a quantitative floor inside the small-numbers gate), fits the selector audit validly but with low power, does not fit conformal-martingale drift detection better than a simpler tool, and must NOT be used to create sub-grades or to feed the orchestrator's reward.

### S1 · Betting e-process for "P fails with prob ≥ ε under D" — Waudby-Smith & Ramdas (JRSS-B 2024) + SAVI survey
**Mechanism (exact).** Experimentalist draws x_1, x_2, … from a declared distribution D over family F; X_i := 1[P fails on x_i]. Null H0_ε: E[X_t | F_{t−1}] ≥ ε for all t (fail-rate under D at least ε). E-process
  E_t = ∏_{i≤t} (1 − λ_i (X_i − ε)),  λ_i predictable, λ_i ∈ [0, 1/(1−ε)].
Under any P ∈ H0_ε, E[1 − λ_i(X_i − ε) | F_{i−1}] = 1 − λ_i(p_i − ε) ≤ 1, so E_t is a test supermartingale (SAVI eq. (13) single-round conditional e-variable; WSR eq. (29) capital process with Prop. 2/3 universal representation), and Ville gives sup_{P∈H0} P(∃t: E_t ≥ 1/α) ≤ α (SAVI §2.5 eq. (9)–(10)). Max bet λ = 1/(1−ε): E_n = (1−ε)^{−n} on a clean prefix and 0 at the first failure; crossing 1/α needs n* = ln(1/α)/(−ln(1−ε)) clean cases (α=.05, ε=.01 → 299; ε=.001 → 2995). Never-busting aKelly bet (confseq `betting_strategies.py:73-108`) reaches 20 after median 654 clean cases at ε=.01 (my Monte Carlo, `scratchpad/eproc_check.py`: boundary false-alarm 0.049 max-bet, 0.017 aKelly; α=.05).
**What it does NOT license.** (a) Nothing about universality — it is a statement about D, never about F; a deliberate/structured counterexample hunt is a separate obligation. (b) Never PROVEN. (c) Valid only if D is declared before sampling and the Experimentalist cannot re-choose D after seeing failures; WSR §4.3 shows the guarantee survives an adaptive adversary only when the conditional mean constraint holds each round. (d) Optional stopping is free; optional *switching of D* is not.
**Home.** Calibration layer / small-numbers gate: the gate keeps its text ("CONJECTURE until larger cases + counterexample hunt") and gains a quantitative floor: the claim records (D, ε, α, seed, case hashes) in its repro node and E_t; the gate may require E_t ≥ 1/α for a declared (D, ε) before Tier-2 compute on proving — a resource gate, not a truth gate.
**Cost.** ~30 lines numpy, no dependency; literacy = "this is about D, not F"; failure surface = misreading E≥20 as universality, and D chosen post hoc.
**Evidence.** PROVEN-in-source: WSR §4 eq. (29), Prop. 2 (p.13–14), Prop. 3, §4.3; SAVI §2.2 eq. (13), §2.4 eq. (6), §2.5 eq. (9)–(10); MC agrees. Implementation reference: confseq @5ffe733 `src/confseq/betting.py:14-190` (`multiplicand_negative = 1 − λ(x − μ_t)`, cumprod), `test/python_tests/test_betting.py` (crossing-probability test).
**Epistemics risk.** The orchestrator must not own D (it would tune D toward clean cases — strategy editing truth). Fence: D is part of the claim, hashed, immutable per e-process; a new D = a new claim.

### S2 · E-level → sub-grade? — Shafer (JRSS-A 2021), Vovk–Wang (AoS 2021): NO
Shafer §3: any p↔betting-score translation is "fundamentally arbitrary"; his convention (×5 merits attention, ×100–1000 conclusive, eq. (6) S = 1/√p − 1) is a communication choice. Vovk–Wang Prop. 2.2: the only admissible e→p map is min(1, 1/e). So there is no principled ladder of sub-grades; the only defensible threshold is 1/α fixed a priori. Recommendation: store log10 E (with D, ε, α) as a numeric attribute on a CONJECTURE claim; the four tags stay categorical; E never promotes to STRONG-EMPIRICAL (which in PLAN §7 is an *algorithmic* class: ladder + repro node — a different object). Home: claims DB schema. Cost: one column. Evidence: PROVEN-in-source for the uniqueness/arbitrariness facts; the design choice is mine.

### S3 · Selector audit — Choe & Ramdas (OR 2023) e-process + Arnold–Henzi–Ziegel (AoAS 2023) lagged e-values
Two valid tools. (i) Panel gives greenlight probability q_t (predictable), gate adjudicates Y_t ∈ {0,1} "panned out": M_t = ∏(1 + λ_t (Y_t − q_t)), λ_t predictable in [−1/(1−q_t), 1/q_t], is a test martingale under H0 "q_t calibrated" (SAVI eq. (13); WSR Prop. 2 with m = q_t). (ii) Panel vs a cheap baseline on Brier: Choe–Ramdas Thm 3 e-process E_t(λ) = exp(λ Σ δ̂_i − ψ_{E,c}(λ) V̂_t) for the weak null "panel no better on average" — code `comparecast/eprocess.py:66-230` (`eprocess_expm`, gamma-exponential mixture over λ via confseq). Outcomes arrive with lag h (a problem "pans out" months later): AHZ Def. 4.2 sequential e-values at lag h; Prop. 4.1 U-statistic average eq. (4); their warning that for h>1 the process is not an e-process and the stopping rule needs the τ_{α,h} correction. Home: problem queue/selector audit (M4). Cost: (i) is 15 lines; (ii) needs confseq C++ boundaries or a fixed-λ version. Honest power note: dozens of greenlights per month → E rarely reaches 1/α; use as a monitor + CS on mean Brier difference, not as a swap trigger alone. Evidence: PROVEN-in-source (Choe–Ramdas Thm 3 + proof note A.3; AHZ Def 4.1/4.2, Prop 4.1). Risk: "panned out" must be adjudicated by a gate, not by the selector, or the null is gamed.

### S4 · Conformal test martingales for cost-profile drift — Vovk et al. (COPA 2021), Vovk "Testing randomness online" (Stat. Sci. 2021): weak fit
Mechanism: conformal p-values eq. (1) from a conformity score; any betting martingale on them is an exchangeability martingale (eq. (2)); Ville: P(∃n: S_n ≥ c) ≤ 1/c; §4 eq. (6)/(7) CUSUM and Shiryaev–Roberts; TRO Prop. 4.1/Cor. 4.2 E(τ_1) ≥ c. Fit for "skill's measured scaling drifted from declared cost profile": it tests exchangeability of residuals r_t = log(measured/declared) — detects any change including benign ones, requires choosing a conformity measure and betting function ("efficiency… is often an art", retrain §1), and the plain Ville procedure decays under the null (§4 p.1). A one-sided bounded-mean e-process for H0: E[clip(r_t)] ≤ 0 (same machinery as S1) is more targeted and 20 lines. Home: skill interface cost-profile monitor — a strategy-layer alert, not a gate. Evidence: validity PROVEN-in-source; fit CONJECTURE. Risk: none epistemic; it is an ops alarm.

### Rejected
- GRO/GROW e-variables (Grünwald–de Heide–Koolen, JRSS-B 2024): Thm 1 needs densities/priors; for Bernoulli the max-bet in S1 already is the likelihood ratio against p=0. Prop. 1 (e→p via Markov) only.
- p↔e calibrators (Vovk–Wang eq. (1)–(2)): Cairn should never convert to p-values.
- Howard et al. mixture boundaries (confseq C++): heavier than needed for 0/1 outcomes; revisit for continuous measurements.
- Averaging e-values across conjectures (SAVI §2.9) / e-BH: evidence across different claims is not one claim; defer.
- Feeding E into the bandit's reward: converts evidence into an objective the orchestrator can optimize by choosing D — the one real epistemics hazard here.
- Henzi–Puke–Dimitriadis–Ziegel eHL test: batch, not sequential (highlights only; not read in full).
