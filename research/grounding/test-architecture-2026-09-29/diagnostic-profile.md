## Limits

The events support attribution to their named command or stage boundaries. The nested process, wrapper, step, and test-phase durations overlap and must not be added. A pytest phase duration includes its fixture window; it is not an isolated function measurement. The artifact name is an upload label, not a failure signal.

These traces do not measure GitHub Actions cache-service transfer time or establish cache speedup. Timeout cleanup is instrumented, while normal automatic container removal is not separately timed. The logs provide these recorded stages for this run; they do not establish exhaustive timing coverage, causal attribution beyond the emitted boundaries, or behavior on other runners.
