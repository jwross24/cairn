# Synthesizer own view, round 4 (written before opening any seat file)

Plan under review: `plan-r3.md`. The five weaknesses I consider most important, ranked.

## 1. The clock cross-check has no reference at the 60-bit rung (§6 steps 2b and 3)

Step (3) marks a rung INCONCLUSIVE when a trial's CPU-seconds exceed what its counted operations would take "at the per-operation rate measured on the A/A arm of the same rung". Step (2b) says the 60-bit rung runs the claimant alone: "no baseline and no A/A arm run there". So the one rung where the claimant runs unaccompanied, at the largest size, is the rung where the clock check — the plan's own answer to "the counter cannot see arithmetic done around it" — has no denominator. Severity MED (mechanism gap, not a contradiction of an invariant). Repair shape: the gate-owned arithmetic object's per-operation rate at 60 bits is measured by the gate itself on a small gate-owned calibration run (a fixed operation count, not a baseline distribution) recorded in the table, and the clock check applies at every rung including the 60-bit one.

## 2. "Statement hash" names two different objects (§4 vs §7)

§4 makes the claim statement node an immutable content-addressed node whose hash every tag, `review_verdict`, dispute and `justify` result names. §7 computes "the statement hash" over the comparator's closure (the theorem's `ConstantVal` and the `ConstantInfo` of its transitive closure) — a value that exists only after the gate compiles the Challenge under the pinned toolchain. The human reviews the node (informal + formal source); the kernel proves the compiled closure; the plan never says how the two hashes are bound, so `justify`'s "lean_artifact node and an approve verdict naming the same statement hash" is not implementable as written — a Solution names the closure hash and a verdict names the node hash. Severity MED. Repair shape: two named hashes (claim statement node hash; formal statement hash = closure hash), the gate-run record binds node hash → formal statement hash under a bundle hash, the verdict names the node hash, the Solution names the formal statement hash, and `justify` requires the binding.

## 3. M1 fixture (a) is not catchable under the verdicts as typed (§6 2c, §13 M1)

M1's done-when says every planted failure is caught, "INCONCLUSIVE counting as an escape". Fixture (a) is "a BSGS-beating claim whose ops at 50 bits are ≥ generic rho". Under the typed verdicts, such a method that pre-registers its cost honestly (≥ 1.25√n) beats the floor, misses no model, recovers x, and sits inside the band ⇒ INCONCLUSIVE — the correct verdict and an "escape" at once. Fixture (a) is catchable only if its pre-registered model claims a sub-rho cost (then the in-sample miss is REJECT). Severity MED (a milestone gate a fresh implementer cannot satisfy or can satisfy only by guessing the fixture's model). Repair shape: pin fixture (a)'s pre-registered model (a sub-rho cost claim, so the catch is the in-sample model-miss REJECT), and state that INCONCLUSIVE is the correct, non-escape verdict for the A/A control (k) only.

## 4. The ladder table's replay grade and reproducibility record are unspecified (§3, §5, §7)

STRONG-EMPIRICAL needs "the reproducibility record the §3 policy attaches to the table's own attempt (a second agreeing attempt or a passed witness check)". §3's policy is by grade: Replayable at Tier 1 ⇒ always re-run. A ladder table is a composite of hundreds of trials across three arms and three sizes, each rho trial carrying a Verifiable DP certificate; re-running the whole table bitwise doubles the cost of the most expensive Tier-1 artifact the plan has, while checking every trial's witness is cheap. The plan does not say which grade the table carries, what "agreeing" means for a composite (bitwise-identical under the same nonce), or how the tier gate prices its verification component (§5) at launch. Severity MED (self-containment; the verification escrow cannot be computed without it). Repair shape: the table's grade is the weakest grade among its trials; a table whose trials are all Verifiable is Verifiable and its repro record is every trial's witness check plus a recomputation of the table's statistics from the verified trial rows; a table with any Replayable trial follows the Tier-1 re-run policy for those trials.

## 5. The ladder's cost unit cannot adjudicate a non-generic method (§6 step 3)

The gate counts group operations, and a method that needs an uncounted backend "is INCONCLUSIVE at best until a counted implementation exists". Every non-generic ECDLP approach (index calculus, summation polynomials, decomposition) spends its cost in field and polynomial arithmetic, not in group operations, so the ladder as written can KEEP only generic-group methods, while its stated purpose is the "subexponential attack" class. No mechanism names who builds the counted implementation or what its unit is. Severity MED; the honest floor (BSGS on xP == Q) still works for such methods, so this is a coverage gap, not a hole. Repair shape (only if evidenced): the gate-owned arithmetic object counts field operations as its base unit and derives group operations from them, so any method built over the gate's field arithmetic is counted; what runs outside it stays INCONCLUSIVE.

## Also noticed (lower)

- A trial that hits the patience ceiling "counts as a failure in the success-rate column", but no verdict predicate names a success-rate shortfall: REJECT lists `xP ≠ Q` and KEEP_IN_SAMPLE needs `x` recovered on every trial, so an aborted trial's verdict (REJECT vs INCONCLUSIVE) is unspecified.
- The per-rung memory cap "the BSGS table for that size" is ≈ 1 MB at 30 bits, which rejects any method with a fixed small precomputation at the smallest rung; the ladder plan should own the cap as a function of size, with the BSGS table as its 50-bit anchor.
- §3 "at M0 a file beside the gate-bundle pin" reads as "built at M0" while M2 builds the checkpoint; the phrase describes the deployment shape.
- Step (0) "exponent, constant or crossover" leaves open whether the constant is pre-registered or fitted in sample; step (2b) says "the model fitted on the ≤ 50-bit rungs", so which parameters are free must be stated.
