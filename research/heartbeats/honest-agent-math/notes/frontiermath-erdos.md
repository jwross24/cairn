# FrontierMath Erdős: a controlled baseline

Source: Tom Adamczewski (Epoch AI) and Thomas F. Bloom (University of Manchester), "FrontierMath Erdős",
arXiv 2609.25050, 2026-09-06, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25050. Abstract page read 2026-10-30; the paper body is not read.

"a benchmark of 68 Erdős problems that are open as of August 2026", chosen from 652 open problems on
erdosproblems.com. AI systems must "resolve (prove or disprove) one of the 68 conjectures in the proof assistant Lean."
Five models, $300 budget per problem. "One (GPT-6 Astra) scored 3%, and all others scored 0%."

## For cairn
- A controlled denominator against Olagoke's uncontrolled one: under a fixed budget and a Lean requirement, the best
  model scored 3%. This supports the honest baseline in HANDOFF.md: a run announcing a big result is evidence of error.
- The abstract reports no false-claim count, so it says nothing about fabrication rates under this protocol.
