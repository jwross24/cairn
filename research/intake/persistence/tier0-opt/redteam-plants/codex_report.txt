**1 → The test must distinguish “child recovered” from “pattern remains open.”** Withhold each genuine child from `known`: the solver must rediscover it and refuse corpus-relative closure. For the synthetic plants, require retention only through the windows they actually satisfy and only against an upper bound containing the planted point.

A synthetic suffix match does **not** prohibit every subsequent closure. Its next digit can invalidate it, and a tighter Baker bound can exclude its height legitimately.

**4 → What constitutes failure**

**Unsoundness evidence:** a genuine withheld child disappears or its equation reports `closed=True`; S384 reports closure within depth 4 under a bound containing its height; or an exact cut drops either synthetic point despite its verified congruence.

**Execution/coverage failure:** timeout, crash, missing executable, zero matching output rows, or resource `BUDGET`. The driver reports these as errors, never as successful retention.

The negative controls are mathematically contradictory:

- **Early stages:** `a=1`, with every special digit assigned residue `1`. Every possible units digit is then `1`, contradicting evenness.
- **Late stages:** anchor `27`, `M=27`, `ν=2`, positions `(1,0)`. Its level-96 coset fixes the suffix `027`; a long zero-free candidate is impossible. Require `CLOSED-cut` at depth 1.
- **Repeated-key function test:** the same S288 pattern with `a=1`. None of its special digits can occupy the units position, so every window is odd and must be rejected.
