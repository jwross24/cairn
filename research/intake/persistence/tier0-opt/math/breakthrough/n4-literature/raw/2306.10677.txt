arXiv:2306.10677v1 [math.NT] 19 Jun 2023
                                                BRYCE KERR, ALI MOHAMMADI, AND IGOR E. SHPARLINSKI

                                                  Abstract. Given a monic polynomial f (X) ∈ Zm [X] over a
                                                  residue ring Zm modulo an integer m ⩾ 2 and a discrete inter-
                                                  val I = {1, . . . , H} of H ⩽ m consecutive integers, considered
                                                  as elements of Zm , we obtain a new upper bound for the addi-
                                                  tive energy of the set f (I), where f (I) denotes the image set
                                                  f (I) = {f (u) : u ∈ I} . We give an application of our bounds to
                                                  multiplicative character sums, improving some previous result of
                                                  Shkredov and Shparlinski (2018).

1.1. Background. Given a polynomial
(1.1)          f (X) = ad X d + . . . + a1 X + a0 ∈ Zm [X]
of degree d ⩾ 2 over a residue ring Zm and a discrete interval
(1.2)                        I = {1, . . . , H}
of H ⩽ m consecutive integers we consider the image set
                     f (I) = {f (u) : u ∈ I} ⊆ Zm
of I , where all calculations are carried-out in Zm .
   The combinatorial structure of the set f (I) has been studied in a
number of work. For example, Castro and Chang [4, Theorem 1.1] have
estimated the multiplicative energy

(for highly composite square-free moduli m), while Shkredov and Sh-

energy
           +
          Ef,m (I) = #{(v, w, x, y) ∈ f (I)4 : v + w = x + y}
(for prime moduli m).

standard trivial bound
                             #f (I) ⩾ d−1 H
