                                                   Abstract. Let q1 , . . . , qt be distinct prime numbers. Let a1 , . . . , at be non-
                                                   negative integers and x a positive integer. We establish an effective lower
                                                   bound for the greatest prime divisor of |x2 − q1a1 . . . qtat |, which tends to infin-
                                                   ity with the maximum of x, a1 , . . . , at .
